# Research Experiment Infrastructure

This directory is reserved for controlled experiments added during the manuscript upgrade.

Planned modules:
- `baselines/` — matched CNN-DQN and classical baseline runs
- `ablations/` — reward, graph, pooling, and depth studies
- `generalization/` — topology, size, and hazard-shift experiments

Do not place exploratory one-off scripts here without registering them in `docs/EXPERIMENT_REGISTER.md`.
